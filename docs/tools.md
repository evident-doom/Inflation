# The tools

Every program with a `main`, what it answers, and how to run it: the tools in `inflation.tools`, then the viewer and the learning programs. How learning works is in the README, under "Learned behaviour".
Programs without a task of their own run through `runTool`.
Run settings come from the config file, not the command line (see `config.example.properties`).
This list is written from each program's own KDoc and usage line.
The probes, programs that answered one question about the model, live apart in `src/probes` (same package, same `runTool`, compiled by `build`): BiomeAridity, FloodplainCheck, FloodplainSoil, HabitatReport, HerpCheck, HexAnomalyCheck, HexCoastRings, HexForageCheck, HexHuntersPerPrey, HexPlentyCheck, HexPullCheck, HexReport, HexRiteCheck, HexSeasonsByHeight, HexSkillsFloor, HexThirstCheck, HexTradeCheck, HexVariance, HexWeatherCheck, InsectSeasons, PollinationProbe, ResidentHungerCheck, TerrainReport and WindReport. The rest are the maintained tools in `src/main`.

| Program | What it answers | Run it |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | The registry of baselines: trained networks kept, frozen and named, to compare later changes against and to continue training from (see docs/design/model-compatibility.md). | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.BaselineKt` |
| BiomeAridity | Generates a world and tells, for each class of aridity (rain over evaporative demand), how much land it covers, its height, rain, demand and winter, and the kinds of country it became; then how much land lies far from the sea, and the shares of the dry kinds of country. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.BiomeAridityKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM]"` |
| ClimateCheck | Generates a world and summarises its climate: yearly rain on land by latitude and by position west to east, the share of it falling in summer, land by aridity, and the kinds of country. | `./gradlew climateCheck -PclimateArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM]"` |
| FloodplainCheck | Where the world has river plains: how much floodplain world generation makes, along which rivers, in what climate and in what patches; then (with --years) whether those plains flood and stay moist through summer. | `./gradlew floodplainCheck -PfloodArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| FloodplainSoil | Whether river plains have the richer soil and plants they should: soil organic matter, litter, plants, the silt floods lay and the sodden share, for places by their floodplain share, in dry and wet country, against places with no floodplain. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.FloodplainSoilKt -PtoolArgs="(--world NAME \| --seed N [--width KM] [--height KM]) [--years N]"` |
| GenerateWorld | Generates a world and writes maps and a summary. | `./gradlew run` |
| HabitatReport | How much of the map each kind of country covers, and for each kind of animal how much land suits it: the climate the year round (no month beyond what its coat bears by more than a few degrees), its preferred ground, water within reach, and its food; and in which kinds of country that land lies. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HabitatReportKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N]"` |
| HerpCheck | Probe: reptiles and amphibians by climate class: the yearly mean each year, and over the last `show` years where they come from and go (born, dying out, of hunger, lying up, taken by hunters, and the rest: spreading, fire, the cap), as shares of their mean a year; how well fed they are while out; and the insects they and the birds eat. | `./gradlew herpCheck` |
| HexAnomalyCheck | Anomalies by kind of hex: drinking water within an easy walk, forage offered against taken, and wild boar over their usual density. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexAnomalyCheckKt -PtoolArgs="(--world NAME \| --seed N [--width KM] [--height KM]) [--weeks N]"` |
| HexBandLuck | Whether bands' bad weeks come at the same time as their neighbours' (what sharing between bands turns on): how often bands are short and hungry after sharing, the share of hungry band-weeks with a band able to help within 20/50/100/200 km, the correlation of weekly shortfalls by distance, and people's deaths by cause. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexBandLuckKt -PtoolArgs="--world NAME [--years N] [--replicate R]"` (any hex-fauna-check option) |
| HexChoiceEffect | How much one band's one choice changes the return people are trained on, against how much that return varies anyway. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexChoiceEffectKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--size KM] [--variants A,B]"` (config keys `choiceWarmWeeks`, `choiceNoise`; `choiceWay` and `choiceWayValue` hold one way at a fixed value for the forced half) |
| HexCoastRings | What the land offers people by how far it lies from the sea: land hexes in rings by their distance from the nearest sea hex (1: on the shore), and in each ring the people living there, and the means of what a band weighs in choosing a camp (game, plant food, the camp value made of them, water) and of what it does not weigh there but lives by (fish and shellfish within reach, marsh). | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexCoastRingsKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| HexDialArms | Which of the bands' ways change the return people are trained on at all, from one world: the bands at the start are dealt into arms (a control and one for each way at each value tried), each holding its way for the run, and each arm's return is set against the control's, with standard errors. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexDialArmsKt -PtoolArgs="[--world NAME] [--replicate R]"` (config keys `dialArmSize`, `dialArmWeeks`, `dialArmWays`, `dialArmValues`, `dialArmGroups`, `dialArmStressTop`, `dialArmLeadWeeks`) |
| HexFaunaCheck | Runs the hex world (weather, water, plants, small life, residents, hunters and people) for some years, and prints each kind's numbers year by year with the main causes of death, people against the ethnographic record, the identity census (regions of one tongue and one set of customs, how alike bands are by distance, and meetings and raids by how alike the bands were), and the cost of a step. | `./gradlew hexFauna` |
| HexForageCheck | Where the forage goes: year by year, the land's mean standing herbs, shrubs and trees (kg/m²), the browse within reach, soil fertility, and what the animals took in the year by grazing and browsing (kg/m², from the ecology's weekly rates), so a fall in forage can be put down to the eating or to the plants themselves. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexForageCheckKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--years N]"` |
| HexHuntersPerPrey | What the viewer's "Hunters per prey" layer is made of: the layer's value per hex after four weeks, and for all land, for the hexes at 10+ and 20+, and by kind of country, hunters' kg/km² by kind (and how fed), big game kg/km² by kind, and the small life the layer leaves out of the prey. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexHuntersPerPreyKt -PtoolArgs="--world m455-1 [--replicate R]"` |
| HexLandCheck | Runs weather, water and plants on every fine cell and on the hexes' places (their low and high ground) side by side from the same seed (no animals), and compares them at the end, place by place: herbs, shrubs and trees, soil moisture, the flow of each hex's main river, and the area burned. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexLandCheckKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| HexPeopleLeverage | How far people's score (as the training checks it: people-weeks per person at the start, less their luck in births and deaths) can be moved at all by what bands choose: every band moving at random, to the worst place, or staying; and every band holding one of its ways far below or above the scaffold's (network output -2 or +2: about 0.24 or 1.76 times the scaffold) the whole time, the others at the scaffold. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexPeopleLeverageKt -PtoolArgs="[--seeds A,B] [--size KM] [--years N]"` (config keys `leverageTests`: all, moves or ways; `leverageWayValues` holds each way at those multiples of the scaffold's instead, `leverageWays` picks which ways) |
| HexPlentyCheck | Where the viewer's "Hungry next to plenty" layer is high, and why: the layer worked out as the viewer does, split by kind of hex (islands, coasts, peaks, high ground, the rest), with the hunger by what keeps the plant-eaters off the food. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexPlentyCheckKt -PtoolArgs="(--world NAME \| --seed N [--width KM] [--height KM]) [--weeks N]"` |
| HexPolicyDiff | How differently movement networks choose from the hand-written behaviour and from one another: worlds run on the hand-written behaviour, every choice of the given role is kept (candidates' features, the band's view of itself, the hand-written scores), and each network is asked what it would choose there. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexPolicyDiffKt -PtoolArgs="[--role people] [--worlds N] [--size KM] [--weeks N] NET.mlp [NET.mlp ...]"` |
| HexPullCheck | Whether groups follow their kind's sense of better country (the viewer's yellow pull arrows): for each group kind, month by month over the last year, how often its groups change hex, how far they go, how well a week's and a month's net move line up with the pull where they set out, how far along it they get, how strong the pull is where they stand, and their height. Run with and without nets= in the config to compare the learned networks with the hand-written choices. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexPullCheckKt -PtoolArgs="--world lake-1 [--years 2] [--replicate R]"` |
| HexReport | How well a hex map keeps what matters for movement in the fine terrain: what kinds of border it has (open, hard, passes, rivers, none), and for random journeys across the land, how the effort by the hex borders compares with the easiest way over the fine terrain, and whether the two agree on which journeys are possible at all. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexReportKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--cell KM] [--hex KM] [--png FILE]"` |
| HexReturnSpread | What the training's returns look like: worlds played as in a round of hex-ppo with the networks in DIR, and for each role the recorded choices' returns: mean, spread, quantiles, and the share that are exactly 1 (the group neither grew nor shrank over its horizon). | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexReturnSpreadKt -PtoolArgs="DIR [--worlds N] [--size KM] [--years N]"` |
| HexRiteCheck | What bands' rites do (run once as is, once with rounds=false, same seeds): each year, how far a band gets from where it began the year (km, its furthest), the share of bands that set foot in country of another kind than their own (by each hex's chief kind of country), how many bands hold rites and how strongly, and people's score (people-weeks per person at the start, less their luck in births and deaths, as the training checks it). | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexRiteCheckKt -PtoolArgs="[--seeds A,B] [--size KM] [--years N]"` |
| HexSeasonsByHeight | The seasons by height: for land in bands of height, month by month over the last year of a run, the means of what makes plants grow and green there: temperature, rain, snow lying and melting, the soil's water against what it holds, the warmth and water parts of growth, the share of grass cured to straw, how green, and how much grass stands. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexSeasonsByHeightKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| HexSkillsFloor | The skills floor: how long people last who start with nothing. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexSkillsFloorKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--size KM] [--weeks N]"` |
| HexThirstCheck | Whether drinking water binds: month by month over the last year, how much of the land the viewer shows watered (the mean share within an easy walk of water), how much has pools everywhere, how much counts as dry (under 5% within an easy walk) and how far that lies from water. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexThirstCheckKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| HexTradeCheck | What trade does (with rounds on for the rites at the sources): where good stone, salt and ochre are got (sources), and each year the share of bands holding a year's worth of each, how much of their spears and clothes is made well, how much of their dried food is salted, the useful gifts given, how many rites stand at a source, the share of band-weeks spent in country of another kind than the band's own at the year's start, and people's score (as hex-rite-check reckons it). | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexTradeCheckKt -PtoolArgs="[--seeds A,B] [--size KM] [--years N]"` |
| HexVariance | How much hexes differ from their neighbours: for warmth, rain, soil, plants and residents, the spread, how alike hexes k apart are, local peaks and pits, the spread within a hex against between hexes, and whether the square 1 km grid shows through. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexVarianceKt -PtoolArgs="(--world NAME \| --seed N [--width KM] [--height KM])"` |
| HexWeatherCheck | Runs the weather on every fine cell and on the places of the hexes (their low and high ground) side by side, from the same seed, and compares them place by place: mean temperature, rain and snow over the years, and how many weeks snow lies. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.HexWeatherCheckKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| InsectSeasons | Probe: insects month by month, by climate class, over the last `years` years. | `./gradlew insectSeasons` |
| PollinationProbe | Runs the hex world and prints, month by month, how well the fruit-bearing plants are pollinated, north to south. | `./gradlew pollinationProbe -PprobeArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| ResidentHungerCheck | When and where resident kinds go hungry: for each kind named, month by month, its heads, how well fed, the share of heads and of hunger deaths in places with little water within reach (tether) or little green, and how many heads stand in places their kind barely lives in (the edge it spills into); then the hunger deaths by how much water is within reach, how green the place is, and how many of the kind live there. | `./gradlew residentHunger -PhungerArgs="(--world NAME / --seed N [--width KM] [--height KM]) [--years N] [--kinds a,b]"` |
| SettledStart | Turns a long run's history into the starting state of new worlds: for each kind, where it settled, as animals per 1000 km² of each kind of country, averaged over the last years of the run. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.SettledStartKt -PtoolArgs="HISTORY.csv OUT.properties [--seed N] [--from-year Y]"` |
| TerrainReport | How mountainous country is shaped and how much it steers travel: slopes and relief of the high ground, how much of it is plateau, and for random journeys of a set length across mountains and across lowland, how much longer and harder the easiest way is than the straight line. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.TerrainReportKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--km N] [--belt KM] [--ridges KM] [--ridge-share S]"` |
| TestWorlds | Settled test worlds for short runs, and A/B comparisons on them against the full map; whether runs' histories stayed within nominal limits. | `./gradlew testWorld -PtestArgs="list"` (also build NAME..., ab, nominal HISTORY.csv..., ...) |
| WaterCheck | Runs weather, water and plants on the hexes' height bands (no animals) and reports, year by year, how much of dry country has water within reach in its driest week, how the springs hold up, how the ground water stands, and whether rivers run dry. | `./gradlew hexWaterCheck -PwaterArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--years N]"` |
| WindReport | Runs the weather alone and reports how the winds behave: how often each regime comes in each season, whether rain still averages to the climate normals, how much a cell's rain varies from week to week, and maps of rain in a westerly and a northerly week with the wind drawn on. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.tools.WindReportKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--size KM] [--height KM] [--years N] [--out DIR]"` |

## The viewer and pictures (`inflation.ui`)

| Program | What it does | Run it |
|---|---|---|
| HexViewer | Watches the hex world over the fine terrain, with time controls, layers, a group's follow window and Save…/Load… (see the README). `nets=DIR` in the config makes the learned networks the model. | `./gradlew hexViewer -PviewerArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--load FILE]"` |
| HexHistoryPanel | Opens a saved history's graphs; with `--png OUT KEY...` draws those series by year into a picture instead. | `./gradlew hexHistory -PhistoryFile=FILE.csv` |
| HexPicture | A picture of the hexes over the fine terrain: relief, plant cover, rivers, and each border marked by what it takes to cross on foot. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.ui.HexPictureKt -PtoolArgs="[--seed N] [--width KM] [--height KM] [--out FILE] [--x KM --y KM --size KM] [--px N]"` |

## Learning (`inflation.learning`)

| Program | What it does | Run it |
|---|---|---|
| HexPpo | The trainer: learns the hunters' and people's move networks and the bands' ways by PPO, keeping a role's new network only when the ecosystem check passes. Learning settings are `ppo.*` keys. | `./gradlew hexPpo -PppoArgs="[--out DIR] [--worlds N] [--size KM\|WxH] [--years N] [--rounds N] [--check N] [--check-seeds N] [--world-rounds N] [--no-overlap] [--burn-in YEARS] [--hold ROLE[,ROLE]:ROUNDS] [--no-ways] [--maps SEED:LAT,...] [--check-on-maps]"` |
| HexEvolveWays | Evolves bands' or packs' ways by evolution strategies on small worlds. Keys `evolveWays.sigma`, `evolveWays.rate`, `evolveWays.hidden`. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.learning.HexEvolveWays -PtoolArgs="[--role bands\|hunters] [--out DIR] [--seeds 1,2,..] [--size KM] [--years N] [--gens N] [--pairs N] [--nets DIR]"` |
| HexPickWorld | Ranks small worlds by how hard their seasons push plant-eaters to move. | `./gradlew runTool -Pmain=inflation.learning.HexPickWorld -PtoolArgs="[--seeds 1-40] [--size KM] [--lats 30,36,42]"` |
