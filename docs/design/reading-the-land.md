# Reading the land, and choices that turn dangers into advantages (2026-10-15)

From Olli's notes of this morning:
- The ecology is balanced and every niche is taken except that of a smart, learning primate.
- Dangers should be real, but each should be lessened, or even turned to advantage, by planning, care and skill.
- Work on mechanisms the agents control by their choices, creating the choices where needed, rather than tuning constants.
- Lethality is roughly right now, so new dangers come with the choices that meet them.

## 1. Signs the bands can read (new inputs for the people networks)

Each is a place feature, read for the candidate hexes a band weighs.

- **Kind of country:** tree, shrub and grass cover, and whether it is marsh or rock. Vegetation is visible a few hexes off.
- **Tracks and paths:** herds that passed this season (trails), and the herd heads seen there this month.
- **Water now against usual:** a seasonal water hole running dry shows before it is gone.
- **Dung and eaten vegetation:** grazing pressure this week tells where the herds are feeding.
- **Carcasses and bones:** where killing happens, and so where the predators hunt.
- **Predator sign:** already an input, the hunters' weight about. Bands also remember attacks (built today).

The migrations' seasonal patterns were built for the bands to learn and exploit. These signs make them visible.

## 2. Choices that meet the dangers (new outputs of the bands' ways)

Each starts at the hand-written default (an output of 0), so the model's lethality does not change until a band chooses otherwise.

- **Gathering range:** how far from camp the gatherers go.
  - **Further:** more food (more ground and more of each hex), but the ground to watch grows with the square of the distance. Exposure to predators and getting lost rise with it, and so does the walk.
  - **Near camp:** safe, but the near ground is stripped sooner.
  - **The hand-written default** is today's reach.
- **Watching the children:** how many adults stay at camp with the young.
  - It costs hands for gathering and hunting.
  - Children left alone are easy pickings, so predation and accidents among the young fall steeply with watching.
  - **The default** is today's level.
- **Fire through the night:** firewood to gather (time, and wood near camp).
  - It keeps beasts off at night (the fire guard is built today, but with fire always kept); without it the guard is lost.
  - **The default** is to keep it, as now.
- **Keeping away from the sick:** refuse to meet, marry with, or feast with a band showing sickness.
  - It loses ties, partners and news, but spares the band the cough and the flux.
  - **The default** is to meet as now. The hygiene custom already does part of this as a fixed habit.
- **Moving camp out of a fouled or unlucky place** is already partly a choice (camp fouling, unlucky camps). It becomes visible through the signs above.

## 3. Turning dangers into advantages

These are the next steps after 1 and 2.

- **Scavenging:** carcass signs lead to kills that can be stolen from lions and hyenas: meat, at a risk.
- **Watering holes:** herds come to drink, so waiting there is good hunting, and also where predators wait.
- **Predators' leavings:** following vultures to a kill.
- **Dogs:** they warn and guard, and later hunt.
- **Fire drives and burning:** burnt ground greens and draws herds.
