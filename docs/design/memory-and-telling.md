# Memory and telling

## Engineered memory

The networks have no memory of their own. They score each candidate place from this week's inputs. All memory is engineered state that hand-written code turns into inputs:
- **Remembered places:** each group keeps a 16-slot `PlaceMemory`, read through fixed place features (remembered season, fed, water, danger, age, ancestors, appointment). Bands pass memories at meetings as far as they trust each other (`PlaceMemory.hear`), and tell each other routes by their landmarks (`HexRoutes`).
- **Herds:** the place they did best in each month, and how well. It drives migration and passes to offshoots.
- **Hunters:** the ground they hold, and the hexes they hunted out lately.
- **Bands:** the country they know (exact hexes), their caches, camps and kin.

Communication is engineered too:
- packs read scent marks;
- game shuns ground where its kind was killed (a shared landscape of fear);
- bands that understand each other share exact sets of known hexes.

## Steps

1. **Remembered places as tokens.** Each token is a place plus what happened there: food by month, water, danger, kills, people met. The network reads them when scoring a candidate. The storage is engineered; what matters is learned.
2. **Telling by passing tokens at meetings.** Animals pass a few, and bands pass more as far as they understand each other. What to share is a learned choice.
3. **A learned state carried from week to week**, for what the tokens miss.

## Spoken places are descriptions

A told place is not a coordinate. It has three parts:
- **an anchor** both sides can name: the sea, a range, a river, a lake, a camp, a burial site;
- **a relation:** a direction sector, or across, beyond, along, upstream of;
- **a distance class**, in days' walk.

The listener resolves it against its own map into a spread of likely hexes.
- An unknown anchor leaves only the bearing and distance: "far to the east".
- Anchor names are words in the band's tongue, so bands with drifting languages misunderstand each other more.

**A route** is a chain of descriptions, resolved leg by leg. A band can go astray where its knowledge ends. Walking a route lays down a trail it can pass on.

**Events** carry a time and a purpose:
- **The time** is anchored to what can be counted: moons, seasons, the solstice (sky lore sharpens these), or "when the cranes come". It can recur.
- **The purpose** is a meeting, a burial, a harvest or a hunt.

"On the fifth new moon we meet atop Table Mountain where our ancestors rest" is one place token, tagged as a burial site, plus a recurring time and a purpose. Aggregation sites and sacred places arise from such tokens.

**Animals** use the thin form. A bee's dance is a bearing and a distance without an anchor. A herd's migration route passes from mother to young.

## The split

**Engineered:**
- resolving descriptions into likely places;
- turning counts into dates.

**Learned:**
- what to remember and what to tell;
- whom to trust;
- when to go.
