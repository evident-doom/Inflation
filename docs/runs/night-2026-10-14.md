## Decisions for you

1. **Dogs.** Taming is written but sits behind `-Dhex.taming`, off by default. With it on, the first dogs appeared within 30 years of the first bands living by wolf packs; by year 130 there were 43 dogs in 16 bands. Pick one: keep it on as it is, slow it right down so dogs take centuries, or leave it off.
2. **Hunters and big game without a niche.** These collapse with or without people, so they are not people's doing:

   | Kind | Start | With people, year 100 | Without people, year 100 | With people, year 445 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Leopards | about 100 | gone by year 40 | 46 | gone |
   | Cheetahs | 1,100–1,400 | gone by year 40 | 68 | gone |
   | Jackals | about 10,000 | 23 | 312 | gone |
   | Ostriches | about 27,000 | 4 | 111 | gone |
   | Lions | 1,200–1,500 | 1,100–1,450 | 5,100–7,300 | 380–1,230 |

   Without people, lions take over. With people, lions are held back but the smaller hunters still starve. Dromedaries and wild horses die mostly of thirst. Over the long run people slowly hunt down the open-country herds: wild horses fall from 1,000 to 360 and onagers from 5,400 to 3,400 over 345 years. I changed no species to keep it alive. Which mechanism or habitat to look at is your call.
3. **Two gaps against the record** that would need new mechanisms, not new numbers:
   - Adults at 15 have 16–19 more years, against 35–40. Crowding sickness is about 30% of people's deaths where bands cluster.
   - Violent deaths are 0%, against 2–15%. Hungry bands are now fed by their neighbours, so raids almost never start, and feuds need raids to start.

## What was built

**People, step 3: skills, tools and camps.**
- Bands learn five food skills by practice and lose them unused: gathering, small game, fishing, hunting and storing.
- They make and mend seven tools from local stone, wood, fibre, hide, bone, sinew and resin: spear, bow, net, sickle, grinding stone, basket and clothes.
- Bows, sickles and grinding stones are hit on now and then, and shown to bands met. By year 100 every band knows them.
- Births come closer in a settled camp with stores. Children sicken in a camp kept too long, and bands leave fouled camps.

**People, step 4: culture.**
- **Customs.** Nine customs, each with an effect: burial, sky lore, sparing mothers and young, oral tradition, feasts, medicine, marrying out, hygiene and sharing. They are copied from bands doing better, and taken up or dropped now and then.
- **Words.** Each band has its own word for each concept. Understanding between bands sets how much passes when they meet.
- **Kinship and age.** Marrying out mixes lineages and keeps inbreeding down. Elders and grandmothers help mothers raise children.
- **Conflict.** Raids for food and feuds, with grudges held against a lineage.

**People, more.**
- **Land and food:** caches at old camps; desert kites (251 by year 445, about 2,100 animals a year taken in drives); crowding sickness.
- **Sharing on demand:** a hungry band is fed by a better-stocked band nearby.
- **Knowing the country:** bands know about 15,000–20,000 km² each.
- **Taming:** behind the switch above.

**Calendar.** The year now starts at the winter solstice, so month 1 is about January. Runs still start at the spring equinox of year 0, and every seasonal rule moved by three months. One real change: vultures now hatch in late winter, as their comment always said.

**Saving and loading.**
- `--save FILE` and `--load FILE` in `hexFauna`; Save… and Load… in the viewer, and `--load FILE` on its command line.
- Fields are matched by name, so small code changes still load.
- The generated world is kept in `~/.inflation/world-cache`, so a run starts in about 30 s instead of about 3 minutes, with identical results.

**Fixes found by checking people against the record.**
- Inbreeding killed children about eight times too often.
- Elders died too fast.
- People died of thirst within a day's walk of water.
- Children's ordinary deaths double-counted the causes the model now handles separately.
- **Reserves bug, for every group:** only a week at exactly 100% fed laid on reserves, so hunting packs and bands never built any. Now any group 90% fed or better lays them on.

**Learning.**
- `hex-imitate --people` copies the bands' moves, 92% the same choice.
- `hex-evolve` evolves any kind that has a network in the start directory, people included.
